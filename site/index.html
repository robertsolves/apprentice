How it works

You tell your AI assistant what you want done, in your own words. apprentice lets it use your computer to do it, and nothing happens without your OK.

  1. You ask

    Type what you need, like “download my January phone bill”, to the AI assistant you already use.

  2. It looks

    It reads what's on the screen, the buttons, links and boxes, to work out the next step.

  3. You say yes

    Before it changes anything, it says what it wants to do. Payments, passwords and anything risky you didn’t ask for wait for you.

  4. It does it

    It moves the mouse and types like a person would, at a calm pace. Touch the mouse and it stops.

Install

You need macOS 14 or later and Swift (Xcode or the command-line tools: xcode-select --install). One command:

git clone https://github.com/robertsolves/apprentice.git ~/.apprentice/src && ~/.apprentice/src/install.sh

It builds, connects the agents it finds, and opens the two macOS permission panes once. Run it again to update.

Questions

Is it safe?

Every change is proposed first and nothing happens until it is approved. Risky steps you didn't ask for (sending, deleting, spending) come to you in a small window, or to the chat or your phone when you're away. Passwords, payments and CAPTCHAs only you can approve. Touching the mouse or keyboard stops it, and ctrl+opt+cmd+. is a kill switch.

Which agents work with it?

Any coding agent that can run shell commands or call MCP tools: Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, opencode, Kilo, Cursor, Freebuff and others. The installer connects the ones it finds. It can also run its own loop against a hosted OpenAI-compatible API.

Will sites flag or ban my account?

It acts at a pace one person could keep by hand, with per-site budgets, cooldowns after a rate limit, and a stop on any challenge. Bulk outreach, mass following, scraping and several accounts on one site are out of scope by design. Some sites forbid automation in their terms, so use it at your own risk.

What does it need?

macOS 14 or later and Swift (Xcode or the command-line tools: xcode-select --install). You grant Accessibility and Screen Recording once; the installer opens both panes for you. Linux apps and browser sessions can run in a small Debian VM under Tart.

Where does my data go?

It stays on your Mac, in ~/.apprentice, readable only by you. Logs never contain what you type, and password managers, banking and health apps are never logged.

Is it free?

Yes. It's open source under the MIT licence.
